02 Before you enquire

Six things that turn
a guess into a price.

Put these on the page and the enquiries that arrive are already qualified. It is the cheapest change a commercial site can make.

  • 01Counter length and the run it sits in — straight, L-shape or U-shape
  • 02Temperature: hot, cold, ambient, or a mixed run
  • 03Front glass profile — curved, square or open
  • 04Integral refrigeration, or a remote plant pack
  • 05Finish: stainless, powder-coat colour, or timber to match the fitout
  • 06Site access — door widths and whether it is a trading floor
